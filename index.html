ENGINEERING STANDARD

Evidence over buzzwords.

I aim to make engineering decisions reviewable. Each project case study should help a reviewer understand the problem, the trade-offs, how the system is validated, and what is implemented versus proposed.

01 / DESIGN

Architecture & trade-offs

Document system boundaries, data flow, identity and trust boundaries, failure modes, and the reason behind key design choices.

02 / VALIDATION

Security & delivery gates

Show reproducible checks where applicable: formatting, linting, tests, IaC validation, image or dependency scanning, and policy review.

03 / OPERATIONS

Reliability & recovery

Describe telemetry, actionable alerts, rollback paths, recovery procedures, cost controls, and the tests that support operational claims.

Project pages should be read alongside their linked repositories. This portfolio does not claim unverified SLOs, compliance certifications, cost savings, or performance improvements.
